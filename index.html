OUR HERITAGE

Nearly four decades.
One enduring promise.

Bichoo Ornaments began in 1987 with Shaheen Ahmed Bichoo (Munaji) and a simple belief: a jewellery business is built on trust. Beginning with gold and silver jewellery, the business grew through lasting relationships and a commitment to purity.

In 2019–2020, the next generation joined the journey. Together, the family continues to honour the values on which Bichoo Ornaments was founded, while carrying the business forward for a new generation.
